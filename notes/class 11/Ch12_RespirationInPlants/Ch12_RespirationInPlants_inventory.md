## Scope decisions

The unnumbered pyruvate-dehydrogenase reaction strip on source page 6, the yellow summary equation on source page 7, and the RQ equation on source page 11 are not numbered figures and are not emitted as figure assets. Captions and neighboring body text are intentionally excluded from all six PNGs.

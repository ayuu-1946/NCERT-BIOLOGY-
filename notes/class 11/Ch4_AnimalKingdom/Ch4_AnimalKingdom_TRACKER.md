## §C — CORE NOUN / TERM INVENTORY (1-Z) — FROZEN

Load-bearing terms that MUST survive into the Pass 2 notes (grouped; every term appears in the frozen snapshot).

- **Classification bases:** levels of organisation (cellular, tissue, organ, organ-system), symmetry (asymmetrical, radial, bilateral), germ layers (diploblastic, triploblastic, ectoderm, endoderm, mesoderm, mesoglea), coelom (coelomate, pseudocoelomate, acoelomate), segmentation (metameric segmentation, metamerism), notochord (chordate, non-chordate).
- **Physiology terms:** incomplete vs complete digestive system; open vs closed circulatory system; intracellular vs extracellular digestion; direct vs indirect development.
- **Porifera:** sponges, ostia, spongocoel, osculum, canal system, choanocytes/collar cells, spicules, spongin, hermaphrodite; genera *Sycon, Spongilla, Euspongia*.
- **Coelenterata (Cnidaria):** cnidoblasts/cnidocytes, nematocysts, tentacles, polyp, medusa, metagenesis, coelenteron; *Physalia, Adamsia, Pennatula, Gorgonia, Meandrina, Aurelia*.
- **Ctenophora:** comb plates (ctenophoral plates), bioluminescence; *Pleurobrachia, Ctenoplana*.
- **Platyhelminthes:** flatworms, endoparasites, suckers, hooks, flame cells (excretion); *Taenia* (tapeworm), *Fasciola* (liver fluke).
- **Aschelminthes:** roundworms, pseudocoelom, dioecious, sexual dimorphism; *Ascaris, Wuchereria, Ancylostoma*.
- **Annelida:** metameric segmentation, true coelom, nephridia, parapodia, setae; *Nereis, Pheretima, Hirudinaria*.
- **Arthropoda:** jointed appendages, chitinous exoskeleton, compound eyes, book gills/lungs, tracheae, Malpighian tubules; *Apis, Bombyx, Laccifer, Anopheles, Culex, Aedes, Locusta, Limulus*.
- **Mollusca:** mantle, radula, visceral hump, foot, calcareous shell; *Pila, Pinctada, Sepia, Octopus, Loligo, Aplysia, Dentalium, Chaetopleura*.
- **Echinodermata:** water vascular system, tube feet, calcareous ossicles, radial symmetry (adult); *Asterias, Echinus, Antedon, Cucumaria, Ophiura*.
- **Hemichordata:** proboscis, collar, trunk, worm-like; *Balanoglossus, Saccoglossus*.
- **Chordata characters:** notochord, dorsal hollow nerve cord, paired pharyngeal gill slits, post-anal tail, closed circulation.
  - **Sub-phyla:** Urochordata (Tunicata) — *Ascidia, Salpa, Doliolum*; Cephalochordata — *Branchiostoma* (Amphioxus); Vertebrata.
  - **Agnatha → Cyclostomata:** ectoparasites, jawless, sucking mouth; *Petromyzon, Myxine*.
  - **Chondrichthyes:** cartilaginous endoskeleton, placoid scales, ventral mouth, no air bladder; *Scoliodon, Pristis, Carcharodon, Trygon*.
  - **Osteichthyes:** bony endoskeleton, air bladder, operculum; *Exocoetus, Hippocampus, Labeo, Catla, Clarias, Betta, Pterophyllum*.
  - **Amphibia:** two pairs of limbs, moist skin, three-chambered heart; *Bufo, Rana, Hyla, Salamandra, Ichthyophis*.
  - **Reptilia:** dry cornified skin, epidermal scales, three-chambered heart (four in crocodiles); *Chelone, Testudo, Chameleon, Calotes, Crocodilus, Alligator, Hemidactylus, Naja, Bungarus, Vipera*.
  - **Aves:** feathers, forelimbs→wings, beak, pneumatic bones, four-chambered heart, warm-blooded; *Corvus, Columba, Psittacula, Struthio, Neophron, Pavo*.
  - **Mammalia:** mammary glands, hair, external ear (pinna), viviparity, heterodont dentition; *Ornithorhynchus, Macropus, Pteropus, Camelus, Macaca, Rattus, Canis, Felis, Elephas, Equus, Delphinus, Balaenoptera, Panthera*.
- **Thermoregulation:** poikilothermous (cold-blooded) vs homoiothermous (warm-blooded); oviparous vs viviparous.
- **Jaw grades:** Agnatha vs Gnathostomata; super-classes Pisces & Tetrapoda.

## §E — SUMMARY INVENTORY (1-Z) — FROZEN

The printed **Summary** (source lines 606–649) closes each phylum in one line and MUST be re-covered in Pass 2:
Porifera (cellular level, choanocytes) → Coelenterata (tentacles, cnidoblasts) → Ctenophora (comb plates) → Platyhelminthes (flat, bilateral, suckers/hooks) → Aschelminthes (pseudocoelomate roundworms) → Annelida (metameric, true coelom) → Arthropoda (jointed appendages) → Mollusca (soft body, calcareous shell) → Echinodermata (spiny skin, water vascular system) → Hemichordata (proboscis/collar/trunk) → Chordata (notochord, dorsal hollow nerve cord, pharyngeal gill slits; Agnatha vs Gnathostomata; Pisces & Tetrapoda; poikilo- vs warm-blooded; mammary glands & hair).

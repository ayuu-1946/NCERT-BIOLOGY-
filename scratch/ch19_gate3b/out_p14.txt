• Thymus: thymosins differentiate T-lymphocytes (cell-mediated immunity) and raise antibody production
(humoral immunity).
• Adrenal: medulla secretes epinephrine and norepinephrine; cortex secretes glucocorticoids and
mineralocorticoids.
• Pancreas: glucagon raises blood glucose (glycogenolysis, gluconeogenesis); insulin lowers it (uptake,
utilisation, glycogenesis). Failure of insulin gives diabetes mellitus.
• Gonads: testis secretes androgens; ovary secretes estrogen and progesterone.
• Non-glands: ANF from the heart lowers blood pressure, erythropoietin from the kidney drives RBC formation,
and the gastrointestinal tract secretes gastrin, secretin, cholecystokinin and gastric inhibitory peptide.

S002. The neural coordination is fast but short-lived
S003. As the nerve fibres do not innervate all cells of the body and the cellular functions need to be continuously regulated; a special kind of coordination and integration has to be provided
S004. This function is carried out by hormones
S005. The neural system and the endocrine system jointly coordinate and regulate the physiological functions in the body

S008. The classical definition of hormone as a chemical produced by endocrine glands and released into the blood and transported to a distantly located target organ has current scientific definition as follows: Hormones are non-nutrient chemicals which act as intercellular messengers and are produced in trace amounts
S009. The new definition covers a number of new molecules in addition to the hormones secreted by the organised endocrine glands
S010. Invertebrates possess very simple endocrine systems with few hormones whereas a large number of chemicals act as hormones and provide coordination in the vertebrates

Growth hormone (GH) - the two directions of error
Condition
Secretion
Effect
Gigantism
Over-secretion of GH
Stimulates abnormal growth of the body
Pituitary dwarfism
Low secretion of GH
Stunted growth
Acromegaly
Excess secretion of growth
hormone in adults, especially in
middle age
Severe disfigurement (especially of the face), which may
lead to serious complications and premature death if
unchecked
[NOTE] Acromegaly is hard to diagnose in the early stages and often goes undetected for many years, until
changes in external features become noticeable.
What each anterior-pituitary hormone does
Hormone
Action
Prolactin (PRL)
Regulates the growth of the mammary glands and formation of milk in them
TSH - the exercises call it
Thyrotrophin (TSH)
Stimulates the synthesis and secretion of thyroid hormones from the thyroid gland
ACTH - the exercises call it
Corticotrophin (ACTH)
Stimulates the synthesis and secretion of steroid hormones called glucocorticoids from the
adrenal cortex
LH and FSH
Stimulate gonadal activity and hence are called gonadotrophins
LH in males
Stimulates the synthesis and secretion of hormones called androgens from testis

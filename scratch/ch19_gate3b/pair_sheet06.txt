S001. BIOLOGY 244 The adrenal medulla secretes two hormones called adrenaline or epinephrine and noradrenaline or norepinephrine
S002. These are commonly called as catecholamines
S003. Adrenaline and noradrenaline are rapidly secreted in response to stress of any kind and during emergency situations and are called emergency hormones or hormones of Fight or Flight
S004. These hormones increase alertness, pupilary dilation, piloerection (raising of hairs), sweating etc. Both the hormones increase the heart beat, the strength of heart contraction and the rate of respiration

S006. The gland is composed of two types of tissues
S007. The centrally located tissue is called the adrenal medulla, and outside this lies the adrenal cortex (Figure 19.4 b)
S008. Underproduction of hormones by the adrenal cortex alters carbohydrate metabolism causing acute weakness and fatigue leading to a disease called Addison’s disease

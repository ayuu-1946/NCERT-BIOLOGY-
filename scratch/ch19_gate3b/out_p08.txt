[NOTE] Underproduction of hormones by the adrenal cortex alters carbohydrate metabolism, causing acute

Medulla
Adrenal medulla - the catecholamines
The adrenal medulla secretes two hormones called adrenaline or epinephrine and noradrenaline or
norepinephrine. These are commonly called as catecholamines.
Adrenaline and noradrenaline are rapidly secreted in response to stress of any kind and during emergency
situations and are called emergency hormones or hormones of Fight or Flight.
Catecholamine effect
What happens
Alertness and skin
These hormones increase alertness, pupilary dilation (the source's own spelling), piloerection
(raising of hairs), sweating etc.
Heart and lungs
Both the hormones increase the heart beat, the strength of heart contraction and the rate of
respiration
Carbohydrate
Catecholamines stimulate the breakdown of glycogen resulting in an increased concentration of
glucose in blood
Fat and protein
In addition, they also stimulate the breakdown of lipids and proteins
[NOTE] The one-line list to reproduce in an exam: these hormones increase alertness, pupilary dilation,
piloerection, sweating, heart beat, strength of heart contraction, rate of respiration, glycogenolysis, lipolysis,
proteolysis. The last three are the metabolic names for the breakdown of glycogen, lipids and proteins above.

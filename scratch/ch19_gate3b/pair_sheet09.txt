S006. However, as mentioned earlier, hormones are also secreted by some tissues which are not endocrine glands
S007. For example, the atrial wall of our heart secretes a very important peptide hormone called atrial natriuretic factor (ANF), which decreases blood pressure
S008. When blood pressure is increased, ANF is secreted which causes dilation of the blood vessels
S009. This reduces the blood pressure
S010. The juxtaglomerular cells of kidney produce a peptide hormone called erythropoietin which stimulates erythropoiesis (formation of RBC)
S011. Endocrine cells present in different parts of the gastro-intestinal tract secrete four major peptide hormones, namely gastrin, secretin, cholecystokinin (CCK) and gastric inhibitory peptide (GIP)
S012. Gastrin acts on the gastric glands and stimulates the secretion of hydrochloric acid and pepsinogen

S015. GIP inhibits gastric secretion and motility
S016. Several other non-endocrine tissues secrete hormones called growth factors

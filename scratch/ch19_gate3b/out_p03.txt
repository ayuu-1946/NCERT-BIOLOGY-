19.2.2
The Pituitary Gland
The pituitary gland is located in a bony cavity called sella tursica (the source's own spelling) and is attached to
hypothalamus by a stalk (Figure 19.2). It is divided anatomically into an adenohypophysis and a
neurohypophysis.

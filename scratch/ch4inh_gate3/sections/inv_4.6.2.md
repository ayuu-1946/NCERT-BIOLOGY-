# Inventory section: 4.6.2 (5 rows)
- F284 [heading] "4.6.2 Sex Determination in Honey Bee"
- F285 [opener] "The sex determination in honey bee is based on the number of sets of chromosomes an individual receives."
- F286 [fact] "An offspring formed from the union of a sperm and an egg develops as a female (queen or worker), and an unfertilised egg develops as a male (drone) by means of parthenogenesis."
- F287 [number] "This means that the males have half the number of chromosomes than that of a female. The females are diploid having 32 chromosomes and males are haploid, i.e., having 16 chromosomes." (counts: 32, 16)
- F288 [definition] "This is called as haplodiploid sex-determination system and has special characteristic features such as the males produce sperms by mitosis (Figure 4.13), they do not have father and thus cannot have sons, but have a grandfather and can have grandsons."

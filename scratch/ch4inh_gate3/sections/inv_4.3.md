# Inventory section: 4.3 (9 rows)
- F168 [heading] "4.3 INHERITANCE OF TWO GENES"
- F169 [opener] "Mendel also worked with and crossed pea plants that differed in two characters, as is seen in the cross between a pea plant that has seeds with yellow colour and round shape and one that had seeds of green colour and wrinkled shape (Figure 4.7)."
- F170 [fact] "Mendel found that the seeds resulting from the crossing of the parents, had yellow coloured and round shaped seeds."
- F171 [fact] "Thus, yellow colour was dominant over green and round shape dominant over wrinkled."
- F172 [term] "Let us use the genotypic symbols Y for dominant yellow seed colour and y for recessive green seed colour, R for round shaped seeds and r for wrinkled seed shape."
- F173 [fact] "The genotype of the parents can then be written as RRYY and rryy."
- F174 [fact] "The cross between the two plants can be written down as in Figure 4.7 showing the genotypes of the parent plants."
- F175 [fact] "The gametes RY and ry unite on fertilisation to produce the F1 hybrid RrYy."
- F176 [number] "When Mendel self hybridised the F1 plants he found that 3/4th of F2 plants had yellow seeds and 1/4th had green. The yellow and green colours segregated in a 3:1 ratio. Round and wrinkled seed shape also segregated in a 3:1 ratio: just like in a monohybrid cross." (fractions: 3/4th, 1/4th; ratio: 3:1)

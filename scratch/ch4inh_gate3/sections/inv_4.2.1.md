# Inventory section: 4.2.1 (5 rows)
- F114 [heading] "4.2.1 Law of Dominance"
- F115 [opener] "(i) Characters are controlled by discrete units called factors."
- F116 [fact] "(ii) Factors occur in pairs."
- F117 [fact] "(iii) In a dissimilar pair of factors one member of the pair dominates (dominant) the other (recessive)."
- F118 [fact] "The law of dominance is used to explain the expression of only one of the parental characters in a monohybrid cross in the F1 and the expression of both in the F2. It also explains the proportion of 3:1 obtained at the F2."
